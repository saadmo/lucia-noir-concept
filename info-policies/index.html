Info & Policies

Everything to know before you book — hours, reservations, dress code, and the rest.

Policies

Hours

Wednesday–Sunday, 6 PM–12 AM

Monday & Tuesday are available for private events only.

Location

351 N Fairfax Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90036

Phone +1 213 800 0048 · info@luciala.com

Reservations

Reservations open two weeks out

Tables seat up to 8 guests. Larger parties — please inquire about private dining.

Cancellation

$20 per person for no-shows

Please cancel at least 24 hours ahead. A $20 fee applies per person for a no-show.

Table time

Two hours

Tables are held for two hours.

Age

21+ after 8 PM

Please keep the age limit in mind when making a reservation.

Dress code

Upscale, elegant attire

We ask guests to dress for the occasion, upscale-elegant, elevated, stylish attire is expected. Guests in athletic leisurewear, shorts and jerseys may be denied entry to the restaurant by management. Baseball caps and other hats are discouraged and may be asked to be removed.

Dietary

Halal, vegan & vegetarian options

Dietary questions: info@luciala.com

Parking

Valet $15, right out front.

Metered parking nearby; nearest lot at 333 N Fairfax Ave.
