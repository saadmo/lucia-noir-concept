About Us

Inspired by the Caribbean. Defined by possibility.

Our story

LUCIA is a modern Afro-Caribbean restaurant in Los Angeles, where familiar ingredients take unexpected forms and influences meet across the menu. Our cooking explores what Caribbean flavors can become through contemporary techniques and a willingness to look beyond convention.

Opened in 2025 in the Fairfax District, LUCIA brings that same spirit to the entire evening—from the food and cocktails to the design, hospitality and music.

Familiar flavors, unexpected expressions

The menu takes inspiration from the diversity of African and Caribbean cuisines. Tropical fruit, Scotch bonnet, fragrant herbs and rich, slow-cooked flavors meet ingredients and techniques from elsewhere, creating combinations that feel both recognizable and new.

The result is cooking with a point of view: layered, expressive and open to discovery. It is LUCIA's interpretation of Afro-Caribbean dining today.

Cocktails extend that approach, bringing fruit, herbs and spice into inventive combinations that hold their own at the bar and alongside the food.

Design that sets the mood

Sculptural details, intimate seating and warm light give LUCIA a character of its own. The setting is considered without feeling formal—a place for a dinner for two, a shared celebration or an evening that lasts longer than planned.

The space has earned international recognition, including Best Restaurant Design in the Americas, Best Surface Design and a Gold Key Award for upscale restaurant design. It has also been featured in Robb Report's selection of America's most beautiful restaurants.

Dinner is the beginning

At LUCIA, the evening does not have to end with the last plate. Conversation continues over cocktails, music changes the mood, and the room takes on a different energy as the night unfolds.

Whether you come to explore the menu, celebrate with friends or settle in at the bar, thoughtful hospitality connects it all. The invitation is simple: arrive curious and make the evening your own.
